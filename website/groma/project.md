Lineup Tracker scrapes match lineups, scores and events for Bulgarian Football Union competitions (elite youth leagues and the top three men's leagues), stores them in PostgreSQL, and serves them through a Bulgarian-language web UI. The behavioural specification lives in docs/use_cases (UC-001 to UC-013) and docs/entity_model.md.
